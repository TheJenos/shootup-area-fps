Music

“Dark Ambience Loop” by Iwan Gabovitch (qubodup.net), from OpenGameArt, licensed under CC BY 3.0. Plays in the last 30 seconds of a round. Trimmed, converted to MP3 and level-matched; sped up slightly in game as the clock runs out.
