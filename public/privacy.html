Privacy Policy

Arena FPS · Last updated October 4, 2026

Arena FPS is a small free multiplayer browser game, playable on its website (shootup-arena-fps.netlify.app) and as a Discord Activity. This policy explains what data the game uses. In short: only what's needed to play a match, kept only while the match is running, and never sold or used for ads.

What we collect and why

DataWhyHow long
Your player name: typed on the website, or your Discord display name in the Discord Activity Shown to the other players in your match (scoreboard, kill feed, name tags) Until you leave the match
Gameplay data: position, health, team, kills, deaths, match stats, ping, and in-game events such as shots Needed to sync the match between players Until you leave the match; shot and kill events are deleted after about 3 seconds
Room details: room name, game mode, map seed and member names To list open matches and let others join Deleted when the last player leaves the room
Discord account basics (Discord Activity only): your Discord user ID, username and display name, via the identify permission To sign you in and show your name; the access token is used only to authenticate with the Discord client Not stored by us; only your display name enters the match
Settings on your device: your last player name, mouse sensitivity and key bindings So the game remembers your preferences In your browser's local storage until you clear it

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no cookies.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone beyond the services below that run the game.
  • We don't access your Discord messages, servers, friends list or email address.

Services that process data for us

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database (Singapore region) stores the live match data described above. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Netlify hosts the website and the small server function used for Discord sign-in. Like any web host, it processes technical request data such as IP addresses. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Discord, when you play the Activity. Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Discord.

Who can see your data

Everything in a match (your name and gameplay data) is visible to the other players, and the list of open rooms is public. Please don't use personal information such as your real full name as your player name.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Discord requires its users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires).

Your choices

You can leave a match at any time, which removes your data from it. You can clear the game's saved settings by clearing your browser's site data. To ask about your data or request deletion, message thejenos on Discord.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: message thejenos on Discord.
